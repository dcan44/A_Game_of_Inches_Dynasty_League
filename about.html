About the League

Twelve teams. One championship. Countless bad decisions. Welcome to A Game of Inches.

Welcome to A Game of Inches

Founded in 2023, A Game of Inches was created under the noble principle that 12 people should be allowed to ruin each other's Sundays for the foreseeable future.

This is a 12-team dynasty league built for the truly committed, or at least the people who haven't realized they can leave yet. With Superflex, Tight End Premium, and IDP123 scoring , every decision matters, every trade has consequences, and there is always someone convinced they just fleeced you.

Three seasons in, the league already has its history: Dan, RJ, and Seth have claimed the championship, blockbuster trades have reshaped rosters, and rivalries have begun to form. Some are competitive. Some are personal. And some, like Pete's repeated destruction of Zach in 2025 that ultimately shattered his playoff hopes, are simply unnecessary.

But winning isn't everything.

There is also the Toilet Bowl, where pride disappears and punishment awaits. From pancake challenges to buying the beer for the draft party, the league's worst performers have learned that finishing last doesn't just hurt the standings — it can hurt your dignity, too.

So explore the league history, check out the records, relive the trades, celebrate the champions, and see who is currently fighting for glory — and who is fighting to avoid becoming the next name in the Hall of Shame.

Welcome to A Game of Inches.
